Delete your account

Film Room by Fade Nation · Last updated: 10/15/2026

You can delete your Film Room pick'em account and its data yourself, inside the app.

In the app

Open More > My pick'em account > Delete my account and data, then confirm.

What is deleted

Your display name, your picks, your group memberships and your recovery code are erased from our database. Groups you started pass to another member, or are deleted if you are the only member. Reports we received about names or groups may be kept for safety reasons.

If you no longer have the app

Email fadenation.support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". Because accounts are anonymous, include your display name and the invite code of one of your groups so we can find it. We may ask you to confirm in the app. We will respond within 2 days.

Anything saved only on your phone (settings, your private picks notebook) is removed when you delete the app.

Questions? fadenation.support@gmail.com
