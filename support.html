Common questions

I got a new phone. How do I get my pick'em groups back?
On your old phone: More > My pick'em account > Make my code, and save the code. On the new phone: More > My pick'em account > Restore. If you never made a code, the account cannot be moved.

How do I delete my account?
More > My pick'em account > Delete my account and data. Or see Delete your account.

How do I report a name or group?
In a pick'em group, tap the ... beside a player on the leaderboard, or "Report this group" at the bottom of the screen.

Scores or stats look wrong.
Pull down to refresh. Data updates on a schedule and can lag. If it is still wrong, email us with the game and what you saw.

Help with gambling.
Call or text 1-800-MY-RESET (1-800-697-3738), any time.
