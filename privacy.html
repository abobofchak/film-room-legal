Privacy Policy

Film Room by Fade Nation · Last updated: 10/15/2026

This policy explains what Film Room collects, why, and the choices you have. "We" means Alex Bobofchak & Clayton Forsythe (Fade Nation), the maker of Film Room.

What we collect

If you use friends pick'em: an anonymous account ID that the app creates automatically (there is no email or password), the display name you choose, the names of groups you create or join, your group memberships, your picks (the team you chose, any confidence points and "how sure" level), a recovery code if you make one (we store only a one-way scrambled version of it), and any reports you send us about a name or group.

Saved only on your phone: your favorite team, settings, your private picks notebook, reminders you turn on, and a saved copy of recent data so the app works offline. We do not receive these.

Technical data: when your phone loads data or talks to our database, our hosting providers receive your IP address and basic device and request information, as happens with any internet service. They may keep logs for security and operations.

We do not collect: your location, contacts, photos, payment details, email address, or an advertising ID. The app has no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

To run pick'em (showing your name and picks to the members of your groups and keeping the leaderboard), to keep the service secure, to review reports and keep the community safe, and to fix problems.

Who can see it

Members of a pick'em group can see your display name and your picks in that group. We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. We use service providers to run the app: Supabase (database and sign-in), GitHub (hosting the football data files and this page) and [YOUR LIVE SCORES PROVIDER, IF ANY - OR DELETE THIS IF LAUNCHING WITHOUT LIVE SCORES]. They process data for us under their own terms and privacy policies.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep pick'em data until you delete it. You can delete your account in the app (More > My pick'em account > Delete my account and data), or see how to request deletion. Deleting removes your name, picks, memberships and recovery code from our database. Groups you started pass to another member, or are deleted if you are the only one in them. Reports we have received may be kept for safety reasons.

Age

Film Room is for adults of legal betting age where they live (21 in most U.S. states). We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. If you believe a young person has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.
